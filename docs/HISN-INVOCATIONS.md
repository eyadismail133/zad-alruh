# Invocations and supplementary Sunnah content

Canonical `hisn-muslim.json` remains the 267-entry, 132-chapter book attributed to
Saeed Al-Qahtani and IslamHouse. Its bytes, IDs, references and digest are unchanged.
The browsing classification is maintained in `HisnInvocationCatalog`; it never
classifies religious content by searching a title at runtime.

The selected book entries contain petitions for forgiveness, guidance, protection,
healing, blessing or assistance, including explicit refuge/forgiveness formulas and
prayers for other people. Mixed entries remain whole and retain their original
situational headings and footnotes. Entry 124 is the prayer of Yunus: its implicit
plea through acknowledgment is retained as a documented exception to grammatical
imperatives. Praise-only entries such as 5, 28, 33, 122, 125 and 240, Quran recitation
blocks, and instructions without a prayer formula remain in the complete book but
are excluded from the daily prayer picker. Classification is a browsing aid, not a
new scholarly ruling or a claim that other forms of remembrance are not prayer in
the broader sense.

The supplementary collection adds **16 prayer records and 8 dhikr records** from
the already bundled, SHA-verified Arabic HadeethEnc corpus v1.7.0. Each record shows
the complete, verbatim original hadith, including its narrative and situational
context, source grade, takhrij and source URL. No formulas are generated, translated,
corrected, extracted by a runtime heuristic, or attributed to the Hisn author.

Prayer source IDs: 3056, 3057, 5329, 5482, 5483, 5487, 5488, 5489, 5491, 5878,
5915, 5941, 6047, 6072, 6332, 66218. These cover guidance, faith, character,
wellbeing, comprehensive goodness and protection. Near-equivalent variants 3598,
3596 and 5914 and named historical prayers 65701/65704 were excluded.

Dhikr source IDs: 3414, 4201, 5402, 6211, 6273, 10098, 65004, 65999. These are
actual utterance/practice records with their original contexts and merits. Some
share familiar core formulas with the Hisn book; they add distinct source context,
not a claim that every formula is new. The collection does not create a new daily
ritual, compulsory repetition count or prescribed order.

Supplementary storage IDs are `1000000 + original HadeethEnc ID`, with original IDs
also retained explicitly. The canonical book counts stay accurate. Search, details,
clipboard, favorites and daily selection resolve the same source records; only
supplementary prayer IDs are eligible for daily prayer confirmation. Existing
non-prayer daily selections and history remain stored, with a replacement notice
instead of a new confirmation control.
